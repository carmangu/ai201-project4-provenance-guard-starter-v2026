# M5 label reachability

Three labels written and wired into scoring.score_to_label:

- high-confidence human (0.00–0.35): "We think this was probably written by a person."
- unsure (0.35–0.65): "We're not sure who wrote this. It could be either, so we're not making a call either way."
- high-confidence AI (0.65–1.00): "This looks like it was probably generated by AI."

## Reachability finding

Across all calibration inputs, confidence stayed in 0.56–0.63. Only the
`unsure` label was reachable. `high-confidence human` and `high-confidence AI`
did not appear — the same separation problem recorded in M4, surfacing at the
label layer.

The label TEXT for all three is written and correct. The score distribution is
what keeps two of them from appearing. Not fixed by lowering thresholds;
Unit 8's run_eval diagnoses it.
